Process

Five steps, nothing without your approval.

You watch the video take shape and approve it at every step. The final render only starts once you're happy with everything.

1

A fifteen-minute call

Your product, your audience, where the video will run and what people should remember. You send us your photos or screenshots, your logo and your colours.

2

Three visual directions

Three different moods (light, setting, pace), each with a test frame. You pick one, or mix them.

You approve the direction
3

Script and storyboard

The video shot by shot, second by second, with one image per shot. You know exactly what you're going to see.

You approve the storyboard
4

Sound and preview

The music and sound effects are composed to the rhythm of the images. You receive a full preview, with sound, to comment on.

You approve the preview
5

Final render and variations

The video in full resolution, its web version, its thumbnail and the formats you need. Then the variations: another angle, another season, another offer.

The founder

Why Alacio exists.

I started Alacio for brands and apps that have a good product but neither the budget nor the time to organise a shoot for every new ad. Rebuilding the product in 3D changes everything: once it exists, it can be filmed from every angle, in any light, as many times as needed.

Two things matter to me. First, that every video is made for you: no ready-made templates, no stock music. Second, that nothing is made up: your prices, your offers and your figures are the real ones, or they don't appear at all.

I'm your point of contact from the first message to delivery. Message me directly on WhatsApp: I'm the one who replies.

Questions

What people often ask us.

Do we need to send you the product?

No. Two or three photos from different angles (including one from the front), its dimensions and, if possible, the label or packaging files are enough to rebuild it in 3D.

What about an app or software?

A screenshot of each key screen, before and after the actions to show, your logo as an SVG and your colours. We redraw the screens so they stay sharp at any size.

Can we have a voice-over?

Yes. Our videos work without a voice, so they make sense even with the sound off, but we can add a voice-over or talking characters, in English or another language.

How long does it take?

It depends on the format and the number of variations. The timeline mostly depends on how quickly each step is approved: we give you a precise schedule from our first conversation.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on the format, the length and the number of versions. Describe your project in two lines on WhatsApp or by email: we'll reply with a video idea and a quote.

Do you work with brands outside France?

Yes. Everything happens remotely, by email, WhatsApp or video call, in English or in French. There's nothing to ship: photos of your product are enough.

Are SOLÈNE, Soldo and Mona clients?

No, they're fictional brands we created to show what we can do. For your video, everything starts from your real product.
